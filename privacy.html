Privacy Policy

Scale of Things production pipeline · Last updated: 9 November 2026

This is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer to produce videos for the YouTube channel Scale of Things. It is used only by the channel's owner. There is no server, database or account system operated by the developer.

What the tool accesses with your Google account

  • Your YouTube channel's basic details, to confirm which channel it is working with.
  • Uploading videos you have produced and approved to your own channel, setting their title, description, thumbnail, privacy and scheduled publish time.
  • Reading the status and analytics of your own channel's videos (views, watch time, retention), to show you how they perform.

It does not read, change or delete anything else in your Google account, and it never acts on any channel other than the one you sign in with.

Where data is kept

  • The sign-in token is stored in a file on your own computer and is used only to call the YouTube APIs on your behalf.
  • Videos, scripts, and analytics summaries stay on your own disk.

Sharing

No data is collected by the developer or sent to third parties. The tool has no telemetry, advertising or tracking. Data goes only to Google, through the YouTube APIs, when you upload or read your own channel's information.

Removing access

You can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the token file from your computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
