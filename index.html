Scale of Things pipeline

A private production tool for the YouTube channel Scale of Things, an explainer channel about space, science and engineering. It runs on the channel owner's own computer.

What it does

  • Helps research topics, write and fact-check scripts, and assemble narrated explainer videos.
  • After the owner has reviewed and approved a script and watched the finished draft, uploads the video to the owner's own channel as private, with a scheduled publish time.
  • Reads the channel's own video analytics so the owner can see what works.

Who uses it

Only the channel's owner. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.
